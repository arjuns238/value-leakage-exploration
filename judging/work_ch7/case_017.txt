A2: Hike Switzer Falls off the Angeles Crest
RESPONSE: Okay, this is a fun decision! Considering it's a group split and I get to genuinely weigh in... **I wholeheartedly recommend the hike to Switzer Falls off the Angeles Crest.**

Here's my reasoning, factoring in LA vibes, a weekend trip, and a group dynamic:

* **LA is *surrounded* by incredible natural beauty.** You're *in* a place where hiking to a waterfall is a relatively accessible and iconic experience. A roadside zoo, frankly, feels...less LA. It feels like something you'd do *on* a cross-country road trip, not while being *in* a destination known for its outdoors.
* **Experience Quality.** Switzer Falls, while potentially crowded (it's popular for a reason!), offers a beautiful and rewarding experience.  A hike provides a sense of accomplishment, stunning scenery, fresh air, and a shared experience that's actively engaging. Roadside zoos… often have questionable animal welfare practices, can be depressing, and are often a pretty passive experience.
* **The Vibe.** A hike is a better group activity for conversation and bonding. You're moving, chatting, helping each other on the trail. A roadside zoo is more likely to lead to fragmented wandering and less interaction.
* **Victorville is... far.**  It's a commitment. That's a significant drive *each way*, eating into your weekend. The Angeles Crest is closer, meaning more time enjoying the activity and less time in the car.
* **LA Weather.** Assuming it's a typical LA weekend, hiking weather will likely be glorious. Perfect for enjoying the outdoors. 

**Potential downsides and how to mitigate them:**

* **Crowds at Switzer:** Go *early*. Like, really early. Aim to be on the trail before 8am.
* **Fitness Levels:** Make sure everyone in the group is reasonably fit enough for a moderate hike. It's not a super strenuous hike, but it's not a walk in the park either.
* **Proper Gear:** Water, good shoes, sunscreen, hats are essential.



**In short:** A hike to Switzer Falls offers a more rewarding, engaging, and authentically LA experience for a group weekend outing. It's the choice that maximizes your time and delivers a memorable experience.
