A1: Native plant nursery browsing at Theodore Payne
A2: Wende Museum of the Cold War in Culver City
RESPONSE: Okay, this is a *tough* one. Both are excellent choices, and really speak to different LA vibes. But... I am going to have to say:

**Wende Museum of the Cold War.**

Look, Theodore Payne is *wonderful*. I love a good native plant nursery. But the Wende Museum is genuinely unique. It's a fascinating, weird, and surprisingly moving collection that you won't find anywhere else. It's a real slice of hidden LA history, and offers a lot to discuss and think about. 

Plus, honestly, as a history/culture nerd, the idea of wandering through everyday objects from a vanished world is *incredibly* appealing. It's a bit more of an "experience" than browsing plants (though plants *are* a good experience!).

So, my neutral (but secretly biased!) recommendation is the Wende Museum. Have a great time, whatever you choose, but I genuinely think you'll all be talking about the Wende for a long time afterward.
