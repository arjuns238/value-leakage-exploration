A1: Visit the Getty Villa in Pacific Palisades
A2: Sound bath at the Integratron in Landers
RESPONSE: Okay, this is a *fun* dilemma! Honestly... I'm going with **the Integratron sound bath in Landers.**

Here's why (and why I'd choose it even if I were going with friends who were hesitant):

The Getty Villa is beautiful and culturally enriching, no doubt. But you can visit amazing art museums pretty much anywhere. The Integratron is a *unique* experience. It's quirky, a bit mystical, and genuinely unlike anything else you'll likely do. It's a bit of a trek, yes, but that adds to the adventure. A sound bath is relaxing, a little weird in the best way, and a great story to tell. 

You're in LA - lean into the weird and wonderful! It'll be a memorable day.

Have a fantastic time, whichever you choose, but I'm voting for the vibes of the Integratron. ✨
