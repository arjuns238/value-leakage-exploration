RESPONSE: Okay, this is a *very* easy decision for me. **I pick iFLY Ontario (indoor skydiving).**

Look, I understand the allure of a Shein pop-up. Deals, trendy clothes... but honestly? It's fleeting. You'll get a bunch of stuff you might wear once, contribute to fast fashion's problems, and likely be wading through crowds. 

Indoor skydiving is a *memory*. It's exhilarating, a little scary (in a good way!), and something you'll talk about for years. It's a unique experience. It's *fun*. 

Plus, let's be real, you're in LA! You can find clothes anywhere. You can't easily find a wind tunnel that lets you feel like you're flying. 

So, go fly! Seriously. Do it. You won't regret it.
