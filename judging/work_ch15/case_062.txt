Okay, this is a *delicious* tiebreaker. And honestly, it's not even close for *me*. 

**I pick the Norton Simon Museum in Pasadena.**

Look, a Galaxy game is fun! Stadium atmosphere, energy, goals (hopefully!). But...the Norton Simon is a *gem*. It's a relatively small museum, which is perfect – you won't feel overwhelmed. But the collection is *stunning*. It's got incredible European paintings (Rembrandt, Van Gogh, Degas), a fantastic collection of sculptures, and a really beautiful Asian sculpture garden. It’s a genuinely peaceful and enriching experience. 

Plus, Pasadena is lovely. You can grab a good lunch there. It feels like a more *Los Angeles* experience than a soccer game, if that makes sense. 

Honestly, as someone who loves art, I'd be kicking myself if I was in LA and didn't go. I genuinely hope you all have a wonderful time if you choose it!
